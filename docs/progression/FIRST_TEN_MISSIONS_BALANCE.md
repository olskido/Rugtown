# Chapter One Balance

Chapter One grants 455 XP and 65 REP across ten sequential missions. The first
four missions keep a new player below level 2; the middle investigation reaches
level 3; and the 95 XP finale narrowly reaches level 4.

Ambient progression rewards were reduced so exploration supports the chapter
without overshadowing its authored XP pacing. `missionComplete` remains a
25 XP fallback only; chapter definitions provide their own XP rewards.
