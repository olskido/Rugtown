# First Ten Missions Simulation

The Chapter One mission rewards total **455 XP** and **65 REP**.

The revised curve costs 120 XP for level 1 → 2, 150 XP for 2 → 3, and
184 XP for 3 → 4. Cumulative thresholds are therefore 120, 270, and 454 XP.

The finale crosses the level-4 threshold by 1 XP. Discovery and social rewards
may add small amounts, but Chapter One itself no longer rapidly advances levels.
